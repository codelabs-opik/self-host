Step 6 · Wrap up

Manage Opik & next steps

You now have a fully self-hosted Opik instance tracing real LLM calls. Here's how to start, stop, and reset it, plus where to go next.

Everyday commands

terminal · inside the opik folder
./opik.sh              # start Opik
./opik.sh --verify     # check all services are healthy
./opik.sh --stop       # stop Opik (keeps your data)
./opik.sh --clean      # stop AND delete all data (fresh start)
⚠️
--clean permanently deletes everything in ~/opik. Use it only when you want a clean slate.
